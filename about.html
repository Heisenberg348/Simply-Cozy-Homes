What we believe

How a product earns a place here.

Six rules, applied before anything goes live. Fail a single one and the product does not get listed, no matter how well it sells.

01

Read the 1-star reviews first

Happy buyers write in week one. Unhappy ones write in month eight, and that is the review that tells you what breaks.

02

Compare inside a real situation

A great organizer for a big pantry is the wrong answer for a studio apartment. We always say which one you are.

03

Recommend less

A list of twenty options is not advice, it is a shrug. We name one winner, one budget pick and one upgrade.

04

No paid placement

No brand pays to appear here, and no brand sees our words before you do.

05

We never print a price

Typed prices go stale within days and mislead people. You always see today’s live number, on Amazon’s own page.

06

Pull it the moment it slips

Brands quietly cheapen a product once it sells well. When a pick stops being the pick, it comes off the site.

How we make money

When you buy through one of our Amazon links, Amazon pays us a small commission. You pay the same price either way.

No brand pays for placement or a better review, and no brand sees our copy before it goes live. If a product is bad, we say so and we lose the sale. That trade is the whole point.
