==========================================================================
  HOW TO EDIT YOUR WEBSITE WITHOUT TOUCHING CODE
  Simply Cozy Homes - Section Editor
==========================================================================

You never have to open the code again to change words or photos.
There is a file in this folder called:

    editor.html

Double-click it. It opens in your browser like a normal page.
That is your editor.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FIRST TIME - 30 SECONDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Before your very first edit, make a spare copy of this whole folder
somewhere safe. If you ever mess something up, you just go back to it.

Then double-click editor.html and pick ONE of the two options it shows you.

OPTION A  (use this if you are on Chrome or Edge - you are on Edge)
  Click "Choose my website folder" and select the folder that has
  index.html inside it.
  Your browser asks once for permission to save into that folder. Say yes.
  From then on you can edit any page and click Save, and the real file is
  updated. Just like saving a Word document.

OPTION B  (if that button does nothing)
  Use the file box lower down to open one page at a time.
  When you click Save, the edited page lands in your Downloads folder.
  You then drag it back into this folder and let it replace the old one.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT YOU SEE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Left side  = every heading, paragraph, button and photo on that page,
             grouped under plain names like:
                Top banner
                Shop by Category
                Featured Home Products
                Footer (bottom of every page)

Right side = your actual website, live. Change a word on the left and it
             changes on the right immediately.

Click into any box on the left and the matching part of the page gets an
orange outline on the right, so you always know what you are editing.

Top-right dropdown = switch between pages (home, blog, about, privacy,
your blog posts, the 404 page).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CHANGING WORDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Click in the box, type. That is it. Nothing is saved until you press
"Save changes", so you can experiment freely.

Each box has a little orange label telling you what it is:
  BIG HEADING     the largest text in a section
  HEADING         a section title
  SMALL HEADING   card titles
  PARAGRAPH       normal text
  LINK            clickable text - you also get a box for where it goes
  BUTTON          button wording
  TEXT            small labels, badges, numbers

If a label says "(has formatting)" the box will contain some code-looking
bits like <span> or <br />. Type around them and leave them alone. Those
are the coloured words and line breaks.

SEARCH BOX at the top left: type any word you can see on your website and
it shows you only the boxes containing it. Fastest way to find something.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CHANGING PHOTOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every photo shows a small preview, plus:

  "Photo file"       - which file it uses, e.g. images/product-1.jpg
  "Describe the photo" - a short description. Google reads this. Write what
                       is actually in the picture, e.g. "woven basket with
                       a folded cream throw blanket".

In Option A there is also an upload box. Pick a photo from your computer
and the editor copies it into the images folder and points the page at it.
Done.

In Option B, put your photo into the images folder yourself, then type its
file name in the "Photo file" box.

Photo tips:
  - Keep photos under about 400 KB or your site gets slow.
  - Wide photos for banners, square-ish for product cards.
  - Use simple file names: no spaces, no capitals. under-sink-organizer.jpg


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PRODUCT LINKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every product button is a LINK field. Paste the correct product or checkout link into the "Where this link goes" box.

Add prices only when they are current and connected to your real ordering setup. The site is written to avoid prices on purpose.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHEN YOU ARE DONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Click "Save changes".
2. Close the editor.
3. Double-click index.html to look at the real site.
4. Happy with it? Upload the folder to Netlify again and the live site
   updates.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. WHAT THE EDITOR CANNOT DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Be clear on this so nothing surprises you:

  - It changes words, links and photos. It does not move sections around
    or change colours, spacing or fonts. Ask me for those.
  - It does not create a new blog post from scratch. Ask me, or follow the
    "add a blog post" steps in READ-ME-FIRST.txt.
  - It does not touch the live website. It edits your local copy, and you
    upload afterwards.

And one safety note: editor.html is only a tool for you. If it ends up on
the live site it does nothing there, but you can delete editor.html and
assets/js/editor.js before uploading if you prefer a clean folder.
