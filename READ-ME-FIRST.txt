HOW TO LOOK AT IT ON YOUR OWN COMPUTER
--------------------------------------
1. Right-click the zip -> "Extract All..." -> Extract.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser.
4. Click around the menu. Click any article card on the Blog page - it now
   opens a real article page.

Do NOT type the file name into the browser address bar. Just double-click
the file.


HOW TO PUT IT ON NETLIFY
------------------------
1. Go to app.netlify.com and sign up (free).
2. Click "Add new site" -> "Deploy manually".
3. Drag the WHOLE extracted folder onto the box that says drop your site
   folder here. Not the zip. Not the individual files. The folder.
4. Netlify gives you an address like random-name-123.netlify.app.
5. In Site configuration -> Change site name, rename it to something like
   simplycozyhomes.netlify.app.

When you change something later, drag the folder onto the same site again
(Deploys tab -> "Deploy manually"). It replaces the old version.


HOW TO ADD A NEW BLOG POST (this is the important one)
-----------------------------------------------------
STEP 1. Copy post-TEMPLATE.html and rename the copy. Use lowercase words
        with dashes and no spaces, for example:
            best-bath-towels.html
        That file name becomes the web address, so keep it short and clear.

STEP 2. Open your new file in Notepad (right-click -> Open with -> Notepad)
        and fill in the parts written in CAPITAL LETTERS:
            PUT YOUR ARTICLE TITLE HERE   (appears twice: title tag + heading)
            YOUR FIRST HEADING            (and the second, third...)
            PUT THE DATE HERE
            PASTE YOUR AMAZON LINK HERE
            REPLACE: images/post-1.jpg    (point it at your own photo)
        Where it says WRITE MORE HERE, add as many lines as you want in this
        shape:
            <p>Your paragraph of text.</p>

STEP 3. Tell the blog page the new article exists. Open blog.html, find the
        line that says:
            <div class="posts" id="blog-posts">
        Copy any one of the six <article class="post" ...> blocks below it,
        paste it directly underneath, then change in your copy:
          - data-cat="kitchen"  -> the category (kitchen, storage, decor,
                                   bedding, cleaning)
          - href="..."          -> your new file name
          - the badge, the read time, the title, the one-line summary
          - the image file name

STEP 4. Add the new address to sitemap.xml (one line, copy the shape of the
        others). Optional but good for Google.

That is the whole process. The category buttons and the search box pick up
new articles automatically - you do not have to touch the code for those.


HOW TO CHANGE COLOURS OR FONTS
------------------------------
Open assets/css/styles.css. The very top block is called YOUR SETTINGS.
Change a colour there once and it changes on every page of the site.


HOW TO SWAP A PHOTO
-------------------
Easiest way: put your photo in the images folder and give it the EXACT same
name as the one you are replacing (for example images/hero.jpg). Nothing in
the HTML needs to change.

Sizes that look right:
  hero.jpg .............. wide, about 1600 x 1000
  product-1..6.jpg ...... square, about 800 x 800
  post-1..6.jpg ......... wide, about 1200 x 800
  about-wide.jpg ........ very wide, about 1600 x 900


ONE FIND-AND-REPLACE TO DO AFTER YOU DEPLOY
-------------------------------------------
Every page contains the placeholder lustrous-lamington-190745.netlify.app. Once you know
your real Netlify address, open each file in Notepad and use Edit -> Replace
to swap lustrous-lamington-190745.netlify.app for your real address. Also do it in
sitemap.xml and robots.txt. This only affects Google and social previews,
not how the site looks.

WHAT IS STILL A PLACEHOLDER
---------------------------
1. Amazon links - every "See it on Amazon" button currently goes nowhere.
2. Star ratings say "Add rating". Copy the real ones from Amazon.
3. The three grey chips on each product card are my guesses.
4. Your contact email. It says info@simplycozyhomes.com everywhere.
5. All photos are stand-ins I generated.
6. Article dates say "Coming soon".
7. The six articles have real openings and headings, but each section is a
   starting paragraph, not a finished article. Replace or extend them.


TWO AMAZON RULES THAT MATTER
----------------------------
- Never type prices by hand. Prices change, and Amazon's terms forbid it.
  That is why cards say "Live on Amazon".
- Keep the "As an Amazon Associate..." line at the top of every page and the
  longer paragraph in the footer. Amazon requires both.
