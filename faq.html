Questions & answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Simple answers about products, orders, delivery and returns.

About Simply Cozy Homes

Is Simply Cozy Homes a store?

Yes. Simply Cozy Homes offers home decor and everyday essentials selected for practical, comfortable spaces.

What kinds of products do you carry?

The collection centers on kitchen essentials, storage, decor, lighting, cleaning, bedding and bath.

Why is the collection focused?

A smaller collection makes it easier to compare useful differences instead of scrolling through dozens of near-identical items.

Choosing A Product

How do I know whether a product suits my space?

Check the dimensions, materials, care details and intended use on the product page. Measure your space before ordering.

If you are unsure, contact us with the item name and your measurements.

How do you choose products?

We start with real rooms and routines, then compare function, scale, materials, care and visual fit.

You can read the full process on our how we choose page.

Are products suitable for rentals and small homes?

Many items are selected with smaller spaces and renters in mind. Check mounting, surface and size requirements before ordering.

Do you update the collection?

Yes. Items can be changed or removed when availability, quality or usefulness no longer matches the collection.

Where can I see the price?

Current pricing appears with the product when ordering is available. Review the item page for the latest details.

Prices And Orders

What payment methods can I use?

Available payment options are shown during checkout. Never send payment card details by email.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Contact us as soon as possible with your order reference. Changes are not guaranteed once processing or shipping has started.

Where do you deliver?

Available destinations, delivery choices and costs are shown before an order is completed.

Delivery And Returns

How can I track my order?

When tracking is available, the details are sent after dispatch. Carrier updates may take a short time to appear.

What if my item arrives damaged or incorrect?

Contact us with your order reference and clear photos of the item and packaging. We will review the issue and explain the available next step.

How do returns work?

Contact us before sending an item back. We will confirm eligibility and provide the correct return instructions.

See Shipping & Returns for the current process.

Getting in touch

Can I suggest a product?

Yes. Tell us what you would like to see and why it would be useful in a real home.

I found incorrect information. What now?

Send us the page, the specific detail and what you believe is correct. We will review it and update the site when needed.

How do I contact you?

Email info@simplycozyhomes.com and include the product name or order reference when relevant.

Still stuck on something? Email the editors and we’ll answer you directly.
