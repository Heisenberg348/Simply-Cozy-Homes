Customer care

Shipping & Returns

Effective date: 3 September 2026

Simply Cozy Homes wants delivery and returns to feel clear. The current options for an item are shown before an order is completed.

Delivery Options

Available destinations, delivery methods and costs are shown during checkout. Delivery estimates begin after an order is accepted and are not guarantees.

Order Tracking

When tracking is available, details are sent after dispatch. Carriers may take a short time to display the first update.

Address Changes

Check your delivery address carefully before ordering. Contact us as soon as possible if something is wrong; changes may not be possible after processing or dispatch.

Damaged Or Incorrect Items

Contact info@simplycozyhomes.com with your order reference and clear photos of the item and packaging. Keep all packaging until the issue has been reviewed.

Starting A Return

Contact us before sending an item back. We will confirm whether it is eligible and provide the correct return instructions. Items sent without instructions may be delayed or refused.

Return Condition

Unless an item is faulty or damaged, it must be unused, complete and in its original condition and packaging. Some items may be excluded for hygiene, safety or final-sale reasons; any exclusion will be shown with the product.

Refunds

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method after the returned item is received and checked. Your bank or payment provider controls how quickly the credit appears.

Questions

Email info@simplycozyhomes.com with the product name or order reference. Never send full payment card information by email.
