Contact Us

One inbox, two people reading it. We answer everything that isn’t a pitch.

The fastest way to reach us is email. Write to info@simplycozyhomes.com and put the topic in the subject line so it lands in the right pile. We read messages on weekday mornings and usually reply within two to three business days. If a week goes by with no answer, check your spam folder and then send it again — we’d rather get a duplicate than miss you.

What we’re genuinely happy to hear about

A product you think belongs on the site

This is our favorite kind of email. Tell us what you bought, how long you’ve had it, and the thing about it that surprised you — good or bad. Long-term ownership is exactly the information that’s missing from most reviews, and it’s the reason a reader tip often beats an hour of research. We don’t promise to list it, but we do look at every single one.

Something we got wrong

If a measurement is off, a model number has changed, or a pick has quietly gotten worse since we wrote about it, we want to know. Point us at the specific line and we’ll verify it ourselves. When we change something meaningful, we note the update on the page rather than editing quietly.

A question about a recommendation

“Will this fit a cabinet that’s 14 inches deep?” and “Is the cheaper version the same thing?” are good questions, and we’ll give you a straight answer even when the answer is that we don’t know. Include your room size, your budget, and whether you rent or own — those three things change our advice more than anything else.

Accessibility problems

If any part of this site is hard to read, hard to navigate with a keyboard, or fights with your screen reader, that’s a bug and we’ll fix it. Tell us the page and the device you’re on.

What we can’t help with

Orders, deliveries, returns and refunds. We don’t sell anything and we never handle your payment. Every purchase you make happens on Amazon, under Amazon’s terms, with Amazon’s customer service. If a box hasn’t arrived or you need to send something back, sign in to your Amazon account and go to Your Orders — that’s the only place it can be sorted out. We have no visibility into your order and no way to look it up.

We also don’t take paid placements, sponsored posts, link insertions, or “quick guest article” submissions. Those emails get deleted unread, so please save yourself the effort. Nothing on this site can be bought, at any price, which is the whole point of it.

For brands and PR

You’re welcome to send product information, spec sheets, or a note about a design change on something we already cover. Factual corrections from a manufacturer are useful and we’ll act on them. What we won’t do is agree to coverage in advance, share a draft before publication, or let a sample buy a favorable verdict. If we accept a sample we say so on the page, and we still write what we found.

Privacy

Your email address stays in our inbox. We don’t add you to a mailing list because you contacted us, and we don’t pass your details to a brand without asking you first. Our full privacy policy covers the rest.
