Get in touch

Contact Us

One inbox for product questions, order help and useful feedback.

The fastest way to reach us is email. Write to info@simplycozyhomes.com and put the topic in the subject line so it lands in the right pile. We read messages on weekday mornings and usually reply within two to three business days. If a week goes by with no answer, check your spam folder and then send it again — we’d rather get a duplicate than miss you.

How We Can Help

Questions Before You Buy

Send the item name, the space you are working with and the detail you are unsure about. Measurements and intended use help us give a clearer answer.

Order And Delivery Help

Include your order reference and a short description of the problem. For damage, add clear photos of the item and packaging.

Returns And Exchanges

Contact us before sending anything back. We will confirm whether the item is eligible and share the correct return steps.

Website Or Accessibility Problems

If any part of this site is hard to read, hard to navigate with a keyboard, or fights with your screen reader, that’s a bug and we’ll fix it. Tell us the page and the device you’re on.

A Quick Note Before You Write

For order, delivery, return or refund questions, email us with your order reference. We will review the details and explain the available next step.

For urgent carrier updates, use the tracking information included with your dispatch message. For product or return questions, contact us directly.

For Suppliers And Trade Enquiries

Send a short introduction, product catalog and relevant trade details. Please keep attachments small and clearly labeled.

Privacy

Your email address stays in our inbox. We don’t add you to a mailing list because you contacted us, and we don’t pass your details to a brand without asking you first. Our full privacy policy covers the rest.
