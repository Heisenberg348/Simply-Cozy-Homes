Shopping help

Simple answers before you order.

Quick guidance on products, ordering and support.

Do you sell the products shown here?

Yes. Simply Cozy Homes is a home decor and essentials store. Use the product button to view the latest item details and available options.

Where can I see the current price?

Current pricing appears with the product when ordering is available. Review the full item details before completing your purchase.

How do I know whether an item fits my space?

Check the measurements and product notes, then compare them with your room, cabinet or wall. If you are unsure, contact us before ordering.

Can I return an item?

Return eligibility depends on the item and its condition. Visit Shipping & Returns for the current process, or contact us with your order details.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Contact us with your order details and clear photos of the item and packaging. We will review the issue and explain the available next step.
