Edit your website without touching code

Pick a page below. This tool shows every heading, paragraph, button and photo on that page as a simple box you can type into, with a live preview on the right.

Option A — the easy way (Chrome or Edge)

Choose this folder once, then edit any page and click Save. Your files are updated in place, exactly like editing a document.

Choose the folder that contains index.html. Your browser will ask once for permission to save into it. Nothing leaves your computer.

Option B — if the button above does nothing

Open one page at a time. When you are done, the edited page downloads to your Downloads folder and you copy it back over the old one.

Good to know

  1. This tool never touches the live website. You still upload the folder to Netlify afterwards.
  2. Nothing is saved until you click Save changes.
  3. To swap a photo, use the Photo box on the field — in Option A you can upload straight from here.
  4. Keep a spare copy of the folder before your first edit, just in case.
